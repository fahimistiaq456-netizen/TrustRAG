NORTHSTAR UNIVERSITY — SYNTHETIC UNDERGRADUATE HANDBOOK 2026

Graduation. A bachelor's degree requires 120 successfully completed credits and a cumulative grade point average of at least 2.00. Students must complete at least 30 of the 120 credits in residence at Northstar University. Every degree includes a three-credit capstone course.

Registration and academic standing. The normal maximum course load is 18 credits in a semester. A student seeking more than 18 credits must receive approval from the Academic Dean before registration. A student whose cumulative GPA falls below 2.00 is placed on academic warning.

Courses and withdrawal. A prerequisite is satisfied only with a grade of C or higher unless a course description states a higher requirement. A student may withdraw from a course through the end of week 10 of a standard semester. Withdrawals after week 10 are not permitted except where university policy explicitly authorizes an exception.

Student support. Students seeking academic adjustments or disability-related accommodations should contact Accessibility Services before or as soon as practical after the start of a course. A voluntary leave of absence may be approved for up to two consecutive semesters; requests must be submitted to the Registrar.
